FAQ

Which games are covered?
Fourteen games: League of Legends, Counter-Strike 2, Valorant, Rocket League, Dota 2, Overwatch 2, Rainbow Six Siege, Call of Duty, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, Honor of Kings, EA Sports FC, StarCraft II, StarCraft: Brood War and League of Legends: Wild Rift, with their major competitions.
Do I need an account?
No. Your favorites are kept on your iPhone and in your iCloud. No email address or password is ever asked for.
Is it free?
Yes. Following up to 3 teams, live matches, alerts, No spoilers mode and match recaps are free. The optional eSportly+ plan adds unlimited teams, per-team alerts and app icons, with a 7-day free trial.
Can I watch matches in the app?
Yes, when the match is streamed on Twitch, YouTube or Kick: the video plays on the page, only when you start it. On Apple TV, send the stream from your iPhone with AirPlay.
Is the score live?
It updates at the end of each game (or map), not second by second. To follow every play, watch the stream.
What data is used?
Only what is needed to send alerts: a random identifier, the notification token, your settings and the teams you follow. Details are in the privacy policy.
Where do the results come from?
From PandaScore, an esports data provider.
