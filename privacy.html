Privacy Policy
eSportly

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Version française · Versión en español · Deutsche Fassung · Versão em português

eSportly is an iPhone and iPad app that lets you follow esports teams and players and get an alert before their matches. This page explains, in plain language, what data the app uses.

In short: there is no account, your favorites stay on your device, and the app never gives us your name or email address (only the optional sign-up on this website collects an address, section 6b). If you turn on alerts, our server keeps only what it needs to send them.

1. Who is responsible

ProtoJo Digital (Johan Quille, sole proprietorship), France. Contact: contact@protojo.fr. Full details are in the legal notice on protojo.fr (in French).

2. What stays on your device

This information is not sent to us and is removed when you delete the app:

  • your favorite teams, players and competitions;
  • your settings (alerts, quiet hours, hidden scores);
  • the latest matches loaded, and the logos and photos already shown, for offline display.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera. “Add to calendar” opens the iOS event sheet: you confirm the event yourself, and the app never reads your calendar.

3. What our server keeps if you turn on alerts

Alerts are sent by our server. For that purpose, and only if you allowed notifications and follow at least one team or competition, it stores:

DataWhat it is for
A random identifier generated by the appRecognizing your device without knowing who you are
The notification token provided by AppleDelivering the alert to you
The app language and your time zoneWriting the alert in the right language and respecting your quiet hours
Your alert settings and quiet hoursSending only the alerts you want
The teams, players and competitions you follow, and matches you mutedKnowing which matches to alert you about
The token provided by Apple that lets us start the Lock Screen score, if you turned it onShowing the score on your Lock Screen as soon as a match starts
The update token of a score shown on your Lock Screen, for the duration of a matchUpdating that score after each game
A random token, if you subscribe to the calendar of your matchesServing that calendar to your Calendar app without knowing who you are

Legal basis: performance of the service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Retention: everything is deleted as soon as you no longer follow anything or turn off all alerts, when Apple tells us the token is no longer valid (for example after you delete the app), and at the latest after 90 days without hearing from your device. The update token of a score is deleted when the match ends; the token that starts the display is kept while alerts are on.

The server also keeps counters with no personal data (number of registered devices, alerts sent per day, calls received from the genuine app or not) to monitor that the service works properly.

App verification (Apple App Attest): to protect the service against copies, about once a month the app proves to our server that it is the genuine eSportly app installed on a real device. To do so it creates a random key, which we keep with its identifier for 60 days at most, with nothing that could identify you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in securing the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Services the app relies on

  • Supabase: hosts our server, in the European Union (Ireland). It keeps technical logs, which may contain your device's IP address, to keep the service secure. These logs are kept for 7 days at most. We do not use them to identify you.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and a small file the app reads when it opens, to know whether an update is required. GitHub then sees your device's IP address.
  • Apple: delivers notifications and processes in-app purchases.
  • PandaScore: provides schedules, scores, logos and photos. Logos and photos are downloaded directly from its servers, which therefore see your device's IP address. No information about your favorites is sent to it.
  • Twitch, YouTube and Kick: on a live match page, the video player of the platform streaming it is loaded only if you tap “Play the stream here”. That platform then receives technical data (IP address, device type), may set cookies and measure the audience of the video, and may show its own ads in the stream. It applies its own privacy policy. These cookies are kept in memory only and erased when the app is closed. YouTube is loaded in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

Some of these providers are based in the United States. When data is transferred there, it is under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses).

5. Advertising

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising tools: it does not use your device's advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission. It is funded by the optional eSportly+ subscription.

6. eSportly+ subscription

The eSportly+ subscription is sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment information, name or email address. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether you are subscribed. “Restore purchases” brings it back on your other devices using the same Apple account. You manage and cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Apple informs our server of subscription events (subscription, trial, renewal, cancellation, refund). We keep only the event type, the plan, the date and a hashed fingerprint of the transaction number, which cannot be traced back to you: enough to count subscribers, nothing more. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in monitoring the service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Retention: 3 years at most.

6a. Technical report

iOS provides the app with reports about its crashes, hangs and slowdowns (Apple's MetricKit). They stay on your device for 14 days at most and contain neither your identity nor your favorites: technical data only. They are sent to us only if you choose to, from “About”, “Send the technical report”: you then decide to whom and how (email, message…). Legal basis: your choice to send them; we keep them while fixing the problem, 6 months at most.

6b. Email sign-up on this website

If you leave your email address on this website to be told about the launch, we keep only that address, the page language, the date and a random confirmation code. You first receive an email to confirm your address: if it is not confirmed within 7 days, it is deleted. Once confirmed, it is used to send you a single email, on the day eSportly launches on the App Store, and nothing else: no advertising, no selling, no sharing. Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. It is hosted by Supabase, in the European Union, and emails are sent by Brevo (France, European Union), which does not use it for its own purposes. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box. Retention: deleted within 12 months of signing up at most. To have it deleted sooner, write to contact@protojo.fr.

6c. Website audience measurement

To know which pages of this website are read, each page loads an invisible image from our server (Supabase, European Union). We only count page views per day and per page. No cookies, no identifiers, and we keep neither your IP address, device nor referrer: there is no way to know who read what. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); this strictly anonymous measurement needs no consent. Counters are kept for 25 months at most.

6d. A team's calendar

The “Add to my calendar” button on team pages subscribes your Calendar app to a public address, the same for everyone. We keep nothing about you: no identifier, no list of subscribers. As for any page, the host (Supabase, European Union) sees the IP address of the device that refreshes the calendar, in its technical logs kept for 7 days at most.

7. What we do not do

  • In the app: no account, no sign-up, no collection of names or email addresses (the address you may leave on this website to hear about the launch is described in section 6b).
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own in the current version (the platforms' video players are described in section 4), no technical report sent without your choice, and on this website a simple page-view counter, without cookies (section 6c).
  • No selling or renting of data.

8. Minors

The app may be used by minors. It does not ask them for any personal information.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Erasing your data from our server: turn off all alerts in the Settings tab (called “Alerts” in version 1.0), or stop following every team and competition. Deletion is immediate.
  • Any other request: write to contact@protojo.fr. We reply within one month. Because the app works without an account, we cannot find your data from your name or email address: tell us the “Support ID” shown in the Settings tab (called “Alerts” in version 1.0), under “About”.

If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

10. Changes

This page is updated when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest change.
