About

eSportly is an app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, CarPlay and Apple TV that alerts esports fans before their teams' matches. It covers fourteen games, shows the live score on the Lock Screen and has a no-spoilers mode. No account: favorites stay on the device. Free, with an optional subscription, eSportly+. Published by ProtoJo Digital (France). Data provided by PandaScore; not affiliated with game publishers or leagues.

Key facts

  • 14 games
  • 5 languages (French, English, Spanish, German, Portuguese)
  • 6 devices: iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Apple Watch, CarPlay, Apple TV
  • Public beta open, App Store launch coming
